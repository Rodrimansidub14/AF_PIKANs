## Objective and claim boundary

Close reproducible, instrumented RBF-PIKAN baselines for the four problem families, with canonical references and metrics, then begin mechanism design from measured signals. “SOTA baseline” means a literature-comparable problem definition, reference, metrics and competitive controls; it does not mean that RBF-PIKAN is already state of the art. scientific_accepted remains false until the researcher reviews evidence.

The baseline keeps fixed RBF centers/widths and a fixed scrambled-Sobol training pool. It measures the full path: generated points, PDE autodifferentiation, residual distribution, basis occupancy/sensitivity, optimization, cost and physical checks. Sampling adaptation is a separate later hypothesis.

## Protocol

1. Treat the verifier, evaluation coordinates, held-out pools and canonical PDE definitions as fixed for a campaign. Never tune on the final test.
2. Report one primary metric per PDE: exact/published-field relative L2 for Heat, Burgers and provisional Allen–Cahn; DFG's three canonical QoIs (CD, CL, delta_p) reported individually, with an explicitly labeled aggregate only as a secondary summary.
3. Report physical guards alongside the primary score: max/mean/P95 residual, initial and boundary error, energy trend for Allen–Cahn, continuity and component momentum residuals for DFG, and finite-value/stability checks.
4. Preserve exact training/diagnostic/evaluation point sets and generator seeds. Keep training Sobol separate from held-out residual and field pools. For DFG, use independent Sobol points in the fluid region, rejection-sample the cylinder, and arclength-stratified boundaries with corners assigned once.
5. Keep the comparison budget explicit: same accelerator, precision, optimizer family, training time and seed pairs inside each architecture/sampling comparison. Existing experiments remain labeled screening until the required seed count and evaluation resolution are satisfied.
6. Apply Karpathy's autoresearch control loop after each problem has a credible baseline: one declared training/configuration change per iteration; fixed GPU wall-clock budget; fixed validation score; write every attempt as keep, discard or crash; preserve the best immutable snapshot; never change the evaluator, final test or PDE contract to rescue a candidate. The original five-minute budget is a search cadence, not a substitute for longer confirmation runs.
7. Run two seeds concurrently on the two T4s. Reuse valid finished evidence. The repository's Tier-2 limit is 4 GPU-hours/20 runs; supplemental Heat + Allen–Cahn + DFG work must stay within it. DFG is treated as a baseline pilot authorized by the researcher's current instruction, but its results remain exploratory.
8. W&B receives the manifest, exact point-generation policy/seeds, parameters, curves, quantitative signals and artifact hashes. Local CSV/JSON/HTML/Notebook outputs are the reproducible source of truth if sync fails.
